How to play
  • Phone racket (best): pick Phone under Controls, press Connect phone and scan the code. Hold the phone like a racket handle.
  • Your player runs to the ball. You only swing. The screen shows Forehand or Backhand as the ball comes.
  • Timing aims the shot. Swing early to pull it cross-court, late to go down the line.
  • Low-to-high swing adds topspin, high-to-low slices. A faster swing hits harder, and misses more.
  • To serve, tap your phone to toss (or, on camera, raise your hand above your head), then swing.
  • No camera? Mouse: swipe it through the ball to swing (left or right aims, up is topspin, down a slice, faster is harder). Clicking works too. Keyboard: Space swings, hold ← → to aim, Shift is harder. C switches camera, Esc or P pauses.
