News

News and changes

A dated list of what changed in the game, newest first. For how to use each feature, see the guides.

Written by the Palm Court team

12 October 2026

Paddle cam retired; Hand and Body cam now share one Camera setting

The ping-pong Paddle cam is gone. It needed a bright paddle and a colour lock, so it makes way for the two trackers that follow your hand or arm directly. There are now two camera modes, Hand cam and Body cam, and they share one Camera setting: pick Camera, then choose Hand or Body. If you had Paddle cam saved, you are moved to Hand cam automatically; nothing else changes. The camera setup guide covers both modes, with the same lighting and distance tips.

12 October 2026

Guides, a pros page and this news page

The website now has a Guides section with 14 articles: your first match, tennis scoring, each way to swing, serve and return, groundstroke and net tactics, a plan against each pro, what the three courts change, Survive and Blitz, how progression works and how the game is built. There is also a page for the five pros and this list of changes.

Everything in the guides was checked against the game itself. If something is wrong or out of date, tell us.

12 October 2026

Volleys now depend on how hard you swing

At the net, a volley is no longer one shot. A soft swing plays a block volley, a firmer one a punch volley, a very soft one with backspin a drop volley, and a ball above head height is hit as a smash. Good timing is rewarded: a volley met on time is tighter, and a mistimed one floats and strays. The net play guide explains when to use each.

Rolf Federli also plays a little more aggressively and a little more consistently, so the attacker is a fair fight at every level.

10 October 2026

Couch play with a phone or a webcam

In Couch Play, one of the two people can now use the phone racket or a webcam (Body, Hand or Paddle cam) while the other uses keys, the mouse or a controller. One camera follows one person and one phone pairs once, so only one of you can pick a device; the other player’s cards for those controls show why they are greyed out. If a device fails, the game offers to carry on with keys. See couch play and doubles.

10 October 2026

Doubles: you and a partner against two opponents

Doubles is in Quick Match (you and a CPU partner) and in couch play (two people as partners against two CPUs). The court is wider for rallies, a serve must still land in the normal service box, and a person who lets a ball go loses it to their partner. Pick Doubles in the Match chip.

10 October 2026

Survive and Blitz: two short rounds

The Play page has a new Quick play row. Survive gives you three lives against a CPU that gets sharper with every point you win. Blitz gives you 90 seconds to win as many points as you can. Both keep a personal best on your device, pay a small, capped reward, and are real matches on the real engine. The scoring is in courts, Survive, Blitz and drills.

10 October 2026

A first screen that gets you hitting in one click

New players now see How do you want to play? instead of the menu, with a way to swing already chosen for them (the mouse, a finger or a controller) and a big Play now button. It starts a guided rally with a forehand, a backhand and a serve. Returning players go straight to the menu. See your first match in two minutes.

10 October 2026

Optional sign-in with an email address

Under Settings, Profile, Account you can choose Continue with email. You receive a one-time code, with no password, and your progress, name and leaderboard entries then follow you to any device you sign in on. It is optional: without it, the game works exactly as before.

9 October 2026

Menus you can drive with a controller

Every menu now works with a game controller: the D-pad moves, the face buttons pick and go back, the shoulder buttons switch tabs, text entry uses an on-screen keyboard, and button hints follow the controller you use. See the controller guide.

9 October 2026

Weekly Cup, seasons and a next-goal card

The Play page now shows your next goal, the closest thing you can finish. A Weekly Cup match changes every week and is played with a neutral kit so everyone is on even terms. Monthly seasons fill a six-step track with a title and a racket paint, and court days count the days you play without ever resetting. You can also challenge a friend with a link. See levels, Fuzz, the Store and the Locker.

8 October 2026

Body cam: your whole arm in front of the webcam

Body cam follows your shoulders, elbow and wrist with a small on-device tracker (about 6 MB, downloaded once). Because it reads your racket wrist against your shoulders, where you stand in the room does not matter. If the body tracker cannot start, the game falls back to Hand cam. On 9 October the preview was made steadier, so the skeleton on screen shakes less in a dim room. Setup and lighting tips are in the camera guide.

7 October 2026

The pros now play like themselves

Each pro has habits as well as numbers. Jokavic returns best and lobs when stretched. Navidal kicks heavy topspin to your backhand. Federli slices, comes in behind short balls and sometimes serves and volleys. Sinnari hits flat and early. Alcarraza plays the drop shot and then the lob. The opponent picker shows a one-line style for each. Read the plan for each pro.

6 October 2026

A new sound mix and menu music

Racket hits now sound different by shot type (flat, topspin, slice, lob, drop shot, serve, smash, volley) and each surface has its own bounce. The frame and the net post sound like themselves. The menus have three relaxed music loops with short jingles; there is a Music slider and switch under Settings, Audio. The levels were set by measuring them, so the music stays well under a racket hit.

6 October 2026

Live 3D portraits and cinematic replays

The portraits on pro cards are now real 3D busts of the characters, lit like a studio photo, with the flat drawing as a fallback on weaker graphics. Replays were reworked into a short film with several camera angles, speed changes and an overhead shot for out calls, and big points get a match-point dolly and a low serve camera.

6 October 2026

Couch play: two people, one screen

Couch Play arrived: two people, each with their own controls (left side of the keyboard, right side, a controller each or the mouse), each with their own pro and hand. It is for fun, so nothing in a couch match is recorded.

5 October 2026

A graphics pass and better footwork

The lighting and colour grade were reworked, skid scuffs now show on hard and grass courts and clay has bigger crumbs. Players plant their feet, lean into runs and rotate on their swing, with leg movement that follows the ground.

3 October 2026

The website gets its legal pages

The website now has a privacy policy, terms of use, a cookie policy and a credits page, and links to the game’s Instagram account.
