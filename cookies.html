Cookie policy

Palm Court sets no cookies and shows no ads. It only saves your game and settings in your browser’s storage.

Effective date: 12 October 2026

What cookies and browser storage are

Cookies are small text files a website asks your browser to keep. Browser storage (local storage, IndexedDB and caches) works similarly but stays on your device and isn’t sent to a server with every request. This policy covers both, and explains what Palm Court stores. It adds detail to our privacy policy.

Storage Palm Court uses

These items are strictly necessary for the features you use, or remember choices you make. None is used for advertising or analytics. Each stays until you clear the site’s data.

NameTypeWhat it does
palmcourt.v1Local storageYour settings: display name, controls, graphics, sound, accessibility, and whether online backup is on.
palmcourt.profile (and .bak)IndexedDB / local storageYour progress: level, XP, Fuzz, unlocks, match history and stats, plus a backup copy in case the main copy is damaged.
palmcourt.cloud.session, palmcourt.cloud.sentLocal storageOnly when online backup is on: the sign-in token for your anonymous backup and which scores have already been sent to the leaderboards.
palmcourt.racket.last, palmcourt.racket.prefsLocal storage (phone)On the phone racket page: the last game the phone paired with, and its display preferences.
Phone racket cacheService worker cache (phone)Keeps a copy of the phone racket page so it opens quickly and works when installed to the home screen.

Cookies and third parties

Palm Court sets no cookies, shows no advertising and uses no analytics. The fonts (Google Fonts), libraries (jsDelivr) and database provider (Supabase) used by Palm Court don’t set cookies for us. If you follow a link to another site, such as Steam or Instagram, that site has its own cookie policy.

Your choices

  • Your browser: you can block or delete site data in your browser’s settings. Clearing Palm Court’s site data also deletes the progress and settings saved on that device, and the sign-in token for any online backup, so that backup can no longer be reached from that browser (email us if you want it deleted).
  • Online backup: you can turn it off in Settings. Nothing is then sent to or kept for you online.

Changes and contact

If what Palm Court stores changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions: palmcourt.game@gmail.com.
