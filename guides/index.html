Guides

Palm Court guides

43 practical guides, from your first rally to beating the toughest pro.

These guides explain how Palm Court works and how to play it well: setting up each way to swing, the rules of tennis as the game scores them, how to serve, rally and finish a point, and how to beat each of the five pros. Newer guides go deeper: fixes for the camera, performance and sound, the full controls list, accessibility settings, the Daily Cup, online and ranked play, the economy in numbers, and saving your progress. The glossary defines the terms. They are written by the people who make the game, and the numbers and menu names in them are checked against the game itself. When the game changes, the guides change with it; the dates on each page and the news page show when.

New to the game? Start with Play your first match in two minutes. New to tennis? Read Tennis scoring explained next.
