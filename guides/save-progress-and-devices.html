Your player and progress

Save your progress and move between devices

Your level, Fuzz, unlocks and look are saved automatically, in your browser, with an optional anonymous backup online. This page explains what is stored where, and gives the three ways to take your progress to another device.

Written by the Palm Court team · Updated 26 October 2026 · 6 min read

Nothing needs to be done to save a game: the game saves your progress automatically as you play. The sections below describe what that means in practice and what can go wrong. The tools for moving a save are all in Settings: the Account & data tab (which the game also calls Profile).

Where your progress is kept

Your settings and progress (level, XP, Fuzz, unlocks, what you wear, your created look, match history and stats) are saved in your browser’s own storage, on this device. That makes saving instant and private, and it also sets the limits:

  • A different browser, or another computer, starts fresh. Each browser has its own save.
  • A private or incognito window forgets everything when it closes. Play in a normal window if you want to keep your level.
  • Clearing the site’s data deletes the save. The same goes for a browser set to clear data on exit, or a “clean-up” program.
  • If the profile card at the top of Account & data says “this browser isn’t keeping saves”, the browser is blocking storage, and your progress will disappear when you close the tab. Allow site data for the game, or use the save file below.

The online backup

The online backup is on by default. When it is on, your browser creates an anonymous account with a random player ID (no name, email or password) and keeps a copy of your progress there, updated as you play. It also holds your best scores for the leaderboards, and the leaderboard name if you choose one. The same service runs Quick Play, ranked play and parties. The privacy policy lists exactly what is stored.

A line under the profile card shows the state: synced, syncing, offline (saved on this device) or unavailable right now (also saved on this device). The backup is a safety net, not a second place to play: without an account it belongs to the browser that made it, so it cannot follow you to another device by itself. You can switch it off with Online backup & leaderboards in Settings, Gameplay (it takes effect after you reload the page). Everything then stays on this device, and online play and the leaderboards are unavailable.

Way 1: a transfer code (no email)

This is the quickest way to take a save to a new browser or device.

  1. On the old device, open Settings, Account & data and find Other devices. Choose Get a code. It is a short transfer code that works once and lasts 15 minutes.
  2. On the new device, open the same place and enter the code.
  3. The old save is merged into the new device. Progress only ever goes up in a merge, so nothing is lost on either side: if the new device already has some play, the better of each number is kept.

The code needs the online backup to be on, and it lets whoever holds it copy your backed-up save for those 15 minutes, so do not post it publicly. Moving a browser save to the Steam edition of the game works the same way, and everything you earned comes with you.

Way 2: an email account (optional)

If you would rather not carry codes, the email box in Settings, Account & data (type your address, press Send code, then enter the six-digit code) links your progress, name, rank and leaderboard entries to an address, so every device you sign in on has them.

  • There is no password. You type your email address, press Send code, and enter the six-digit code the game emails you. The same button creates the account or signs you in, and your progress is kept either way. Codes are rate-limited: there is a 60-second wait between emails.
  • The email address is used only to send you sign-in codes. The privacy policy says where it is kept.
  • Sign out saves your latest progress to the account first, then removes it from this device. Signing in again brings it back.
  • Delete account removes your email, your online save and your leaderboard entries, and clears the progress on this device. It cannot be undone, and it asks you to type DELETE to confirm. Export a save file first if you want a copy.

If the game says that sign-in by email is not available, or that accounts need the online backup, use a transfer code or a save file instead.

Way 3: a save file (works offline)

In the same tab, Export my save downloads one file, named like palm-court-save-2026-10-26.json, containing your settings and progress. Keep it somewhere safe: it is the best protection against clearing your browser by accident.

The file is built from an allow-list. It never contains an account, a sign-in, a device ID, a phone pairing code or anything else that tells a server who you are. Import a save reads a file, checks it and shows you a preview (level, matches, wins, unlocks, how many settings) before anything changes. The preview also shows what is on this device now, and importing replaces that, so the confirm button is deliberately a second step with Cancel selected first. Your sign-in stays as it is.

A file is refused, with a reason, when it is too large (a real save is a few tens of kilobytes; the limit is 1 MB), is not valid, is not a Palm Court save, comes from a newer version of the game, or tries to set something privileged. Some settings are never imported because they belong to the device: your swing control and camera choice (a phone needs pairing and a camera needs permission), gear in online matches, the online backup switch and error reports.

Which one should I use?

SituationBest choice
Moving to a new computer or phone, you have both in front of youA transfer code
You play on several devices all the timeAn email account
You are about to clear your browser, or want a backup of your ownExport a save file
You are offline, or the online backup is offA save file
You only use one browser and one computerNothing: it is saved already

If your progress seems to be missing

Work through these before assuming it is gone:

  1. Are you in the same browser and profile? Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari each keep separate saves, and so do separate profiles in one browser.
  2. Is it the same address? A save belongs to the web address it was made at. A local copy of the game, or a test address, has its own empty save.
  3. Was it a private window? Those start empty every time.
  4. Has the browser’s site data been cleared? If the online backup was on, sign in or enter a transfer code, and the backup can restore it. If it was off, only a save file can.
  5. Is the online backup still on? Settings, Gameplay, “Online backup & leaderboards”.

Things that do not touch your progress

Reset to defaults on a Settings tab and Reset all settings under About only change settings. Neither alters your level, Fuzz or unlocks. Your look is part of the save too, so it travels with it; see create a player.
