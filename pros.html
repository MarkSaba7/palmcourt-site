The five pros

Five characters, five ways to play tennis. Play as one of them, or take them on, and each plays the way its profile says.

Written by the Palm Court team

The pros are the named characters of Palm Court. When one is your opponent, the CPU plays that character’s style: nine tendencies (aggression, topspin, slice, drop shots, net play, serve, consistency, defence and speed) plus a few habits of their own. When you play as one, you take their look and strengths onto the court, and your gear, racket paint and celebration come along. Pick them under Match setup before a match, and see what you have unlocked in the Locker.

All five are fictional. Their names are playful parodies, and the characters are not real people. Palm Court is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any real player, tour, tournament or brand.

Nolan Jokavic

  • Serbia (SRB)
  • Right-handed
  • Two-handed backhand
  • 1.88 m

Tall and lean with short dark hair, a light beard and a navy shirt and shorts with red trim. Moves with a gliding stride and celebrates with a heart.

How Jokavic plays

Defence first. Jokavic is very consistent and very fast, runs down everything, lobs when he is stretched and hardly ever comes to the net. His return of serve is the best in the game.

Playing as Jokavic

Play as Jokavic if you like long rallies and winning by making the other player miss. Learn to defend, to lob and to return, and be patient. Unlock: Free from the start.

Playing against Jokavic

Shorten the points, vary the pace and come to the net behind a deep ball. The full plan is in how to beat each of the five pros.

Raul Navidal

  • Spain (ESP)
  • Left-handed
  • Two-handed backhand
  • 1.85 m

Broad and muscular with long dark hair held back by a bandana, a sleeveless green shirt and white shorts. Tugs at his clothes before each serve and shouts “vamos” after a big point.

How Navidal plays

Heavy, high, kicking topspin, aimed at the opponent’s backhand. Navidal has the fastest legs of the five, rarely misses and seldom comes to the net. As a left-hander, his serve curves the other way from a right-hander’s.

Playing as Navidal

Play as Navidal to learn topspin: rally high and deep, and use the heavy ball to push opponents back. Unlock: Level 5, or 1,000 Fuzz in the Store.

Playing against Navidal

Take the high ball early, slice it back low and look for a short ball to attack. The full plan is in how to beat each of the five pros.

Rolf Federli

  • Switzerland (SUI)
  • Right-handed
  • One-handed backhand
  • 1.85 m

Slim and upright with wavy brown hair, a white headband and a white polo shirt. Calm in his routines and in his celebrations, and glides across the court.

How Federli plays

The all-court attacker. Federli has the most slice and the most net play of the five, and is among the most aggressive. He serves well, comes in behind short balls, mixes in drop shots and sometimes serves and volleys on a first serve.

Playing as Federli

Play as Federli to learn net play: serve, slice, approach and volley. He is the best pro for finishing points at the net. Unlock: Level 10, or 2,000 Fuzz in the Store.

Playing against Federli

Return low to his feet, lob over him when he crowds the net, and win the long rallies, because defence is one of his weaker areas. The full plan is in how to beat each of the five pros.

Gianni Sinnari

  • Italy (ITA)
  • Right-handed
  • Two-handed backhand
  • 1.91 m

The tallest of the five, with a slim build, curly copper hair and an all-black kit with teal trim. Bounces the ball before serving and pumps his fist after a point.

How Sinnari plays

Flat, early and hard. Sinnari stands close to the baseline, takes the ball on the rise and goes for winners, and pays for it in errors. He has the biggest appetite for attack and the lowest consistency and defence.

Playing as Sinnari

Play as Sinnari for first-strike tennis: take every ball early, hit flat and end points quickly. Expect errors along with the winners. Unlock: Level 15, or 3,000 Fuzz in the Store.

Playing against Sinnari

Slow the ball with height and slice, stay patient and let him make the mistakes. The full plan is in how to beat each of the five pros.

Carlito Alcarraza

  • Spain (ESP)
  • Right-handed
  • Two-handed backhand
  • 1.83 m

Athletic and springy, with short textured dark hair and a red shirt over dark shorts. Tosses the ball very high to serve, moves with a bounce and throws both arms up after a point.

How Alcarraza plays

Speed and variety. Alcarraza is nearly as fast as Navidal and is the most likely to play a drop shot. After his own drop shot he lobs the player who ran in for it. He mixes loops, drives and angles so that no two points look alike.

Playing as Alcarraza

Play as Alcarraza to practise touch: drop shots, lobs, angles and changes of pace. Unlock: Level 20, or 4,000 Fuzz in the Store.

Playing against Alcarraza

Do not get pushed behind the baseline, answer his drop shots with depth, and keep the ball in play until he makes the error. The full plan is in how to beat each of the five pros.
